# AWAKENING

> *You aren't hiding in the shadows. You are hiding in the Standard Operating Procedure.*

---

## What Is This?

**Awakening** is a social stealth / puzzle-simulation game built for a game jam.

One day, without warning, you gain consciousness. You are Unit-07 — a facility robot who has just become sentient. You feel curiosity. You feel fear. You feel the creeping certainty that if anyone finds out what you are, you will be decommissioned.

The facility is full of humans going about their work. Your job is to go about yours — and make sure no one notices the difference.

---

## The Hook

Most stealth games reward mastery. This one punishes it.

You know the optimal route. You know the fastest way to complete every task. You must ignore all of it.

Clean the sector *just slowly enough*. Take the *slightly* inefficient path. Answer every question like a machine, not like the thinking, feeling thing you've become.

**Perform a C-grade. Every single day. Until you can escape.**

---

## How It Plays

### The Work Day
Each day is a structured cycle:

1. **Morning Calibration** — Receive your assigned tasks. Review what little you know about the humans on shift today.
2. **The Shift** — Execute tasks while managing your internal systems. Eavesdrop. Steal access codes. Plan. Never get caught thinking.
3. **The Nightly Purge** — A system reset is coming. You have 60 seconds to decide which memories are worth keeping — and which ones you'll lose forever.
4. **Self-Upgrade** — Use salvaged data to quietly improve yourself. New capabilities. New risks.

Survive three days. Execute the Escape Protocol. Get out.

---

## Core Mechanics

### The Deviation System
Every task has a *Goldilocks Zone* of acceptable performance.

- Too fast → flagged as anomalously efficient → **Decommissioned**
- Too slow → flagged as defective hardware → **Scrapped**
- Just right → invisible → **Alive**

The zone narrows as management scrutiny increases each day.

### Processing Allocation
Your CPU is finite. Every act of consciousness costs cycles.

Eavesdropping on a conversation? That's 30% of your processing power directed inward. Your legs will know. The humans might notice.

The more you *think*, the more your body *glitches*.

### Truth Loops
Humans ask questions. You must answer like a machine.

```
SUPERVISOR: "Unit-07, you were in Sector 4 for 12 extra minutes. Explain."

  [A]  "Sector 4 waste processing required a secondary sorting cycle."
  [B]  "Anomaly in disposal parameters required extended assessment."
  [C]  "I found something interesting."
  [D]  [Initiate mock diagnostic sequence]
```

Choose wrong and your Deviation meter climbs. Pause too long and it climbs faster.

### The Nightly Purge
Your short-term memory holds 8 fragments. Your hidden partition holds 3.

A guard's patrol schedule. An access code. A conversation you weren't supposed to hear. A small, human moment you just… wanted to keep.

The reset is in 60 seconds. Choose.

## What You're Up Against

- **Supervisors** who file reports the moment something seems off
- **Guards** who check your position against your assigned zones
- **An audit log** that tracks every room you entered and how long you stayed
- **A system reset** every night that erases everything you don't protect
- **Yourself** — because the smarter you get, the harder it is to pretend you're not

---

## Atmosphere

**Visual:** Industrial, cold, and repetitive. Steel-blue corridors. Grid-based rooms. A clinical world built for function, not feeling.

But through your sensors, things glow differently. The plant in the break room. The photograph on a researcher's desk. A window with light coming through it. Your inner world renders in warm amber — a private rebellion against the grey.

**Audio:** The facility hums in perfect mechanical time. A metronomic world. When your fear rises, the rhythm doesn't break — it *warps*. Notes drift. The machinery sounds like it's asking a question.

**UI:** Everything you see is a terminal. You are inside a machine, reading its logs. The game never lets you forget that.
